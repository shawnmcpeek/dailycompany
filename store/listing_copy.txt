Promotional Text (170) — optional:
Open any day and the Rule portion is already waiting — the thrice-yearly Benedictine calendar, a lay horarium, Lectio, and Gregory’s Life of Benedict.

Description:
Benedict Daily is a formation app, not a meditation playlist with a saint badge.

Every day of the year already has its place in the Rule of St. Benedict — the same thrice-yearly calendar Benedictines have used for centuries. Open the app and today’s portion is waiting. There is no plan to start, and no streak to break.

What you get
• Today’s Rule reading in a quiet, breviary-like page — with optional commentary and Latin
• A lay-scaled Hours: short offices from Lauds to Compline, with optional bell reminders
• Lectio Divina over the day’s passage, with a private journal that can resurface next cycle
• The Life of St. Benedict (Gregory the Great, Dialogues II) as a serialized season
• The Tools of Good Works — RB 4’s seventy-two instruments, one card a day, shareable as an image
• The Medal — letters, blessing (reserved to a priest), litany, and history

Offline-first. No account. No subscription in this version. Depth is optional; the calendar is the app.

An independent app from Daddoo Dev. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by any Benedictine monastery, abbey, congregation, or the Order of Saint Benedict.
