# Desert field (later)

Expansion candidates around Cassian and the desert. Not product law. Not an open-now list.

Named PD editions only. Fill `portal-playbook.md` before any pipeline. Do not invent a translator. Do not open a Place house from this sheet unless that is the decision.

**All or nothing per writer.** When a house from this field opens, the whole named CLEAR edition ships with it. No partial year. No leftover shelf. No “we’ll add the rest later.” If the edition is not ready to ship complete, the house does not open.

Cassian is already a house (`portal-texts.md`): Gibson’s *Conferences* (NPNF II.11, 1894), without XII and XXII — Gibson never translated those two. Completing Cassian means shipping the rest of Gibson (the *Institutes*) as part of that house, not as a later shelf. XII and XXII stay absent; that is an edition gap, not a deferred work.

Sorted by how safe the copyright is.

## Worth a renewal check before ruling them out

US works published 1929–1963 needed a copyright renewal at 28 years. Small religious presses often never filed. Search the Stanford Copyright Renewal Database before spending a house on either of these.

## No public domain route

Every English translation is modern and in copyright:

- Dorotheos of Gaza
- Barsanuphius and John
- Isaiah of Scetis
- Philokalia authors: Diadochos, Mark the Ascetic, Hesychios

The Greek is free in Migne. The only English path is to commission or make a translation. That is real work, not a scrape.

## Shape

Cassian, the Sayings, and Climacus alone are well over a thousand daily portions — years of reading from three sources, all of which Benedict pointed his monks toward.

Each writer is one house, one named edition, complete. No leftover shelf. Do not compute a backlog. EB Garamond for the reading.
