Daily Company is a formation app, not a meditation playlist with a saint badge.

You keep company with one writer at a time — a saint or spiritual master and the book they left. The hallway is the front door. You choose a house, then live in it: today’s reading is already waiting. There is no plan to start, and no streak to break.

The hallway is open. Benedict, Thomas à Kempis, Francis de Sales, Ignatius, Augustine, Francis of Assisi, Teresa of Avila, Gregory, Alphonsus Liguori, Thérèse, John of the Cross, Catherine, Montfort, Scupoli, Brother Lawrence, Cassian, and Serra.

What you get
• Today’s reading on a quiet, breviary-like page
• Benedict’s house: the Rule on the thrice-yearly Benedictine calendar, a lay-scaled Hours, Lectio over the day’s passage, Gregory’s Life of Benedict, the Tools of Good Works, and the Medal
• The other houses: the book they left, cut for the year, and the practice that belongs to that life — de Sales’ Bouquet, Kempis’ Cell, Ignatius’ Examen, Serra’s journey and twenty-one missions
• Read Through, if you’d rather take the book as written
• Offline-first. No account.

One-time unlocks per house, or All Saints for the whole hallway. No subscription. Depth is optional; the house is the app.
