Daily Company is a formation app, not a meditation playlist with a saint badge.

You keep company with one writer at a time — a saint or spiritual master and the book they left. The hallway is the front door. You choose a house, then live in it: today’s reading is already waiting. There is no plan to start, and no streak to break.

Benedict’s house is open now. Inside it you will find the Rule on the thrice-yearly Benedictine calendar, a lay-scaled Hours, Lectio over the day’s passage, Gregory’s Life of Benedict, the Tools of Good Works, and the Medal. Other houses — Teresa, de Sales, à Kempis, Thérèse, and more — will open as their books are ready.

What you get in Benedict’s house
• Today’s Rule reading on a quiet, breviary-like page — optional commentary and Latin
• A lay-scaled Hours: short offices from Lauds to Compline, with optional bell reminders
• Lectio Divina over the day’s passage, with a private journal that can resurface next cycle
• The Life of St. Benedict (Gregory the Great, Dialogues II) as a serialized season
• The Tools of Good Works — RB 4’s seventy-two instruments, one card a day, shareable as an image
• The Medal — letters, blessing (reserved to a priest), litany, and history

Offline-first. No account. No subscription in this version. Depth is optional; the house is the app.
